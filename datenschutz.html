Kurzfassung

Keine Nutzerkonten, kein Login, keine Registrierung, keine E-Mail-Abfrage in der App. Keine Server-Anbindung für die Kernfunktionen (Aufgaben, Punkte, Verlauf, Einstellungen) – alles liegt ausschließlich lokal auf dem Gerät. Keine Analytics-, Crash-Reporting- oder Werbe-SDKs. Die einzige ausgehende Verbindung läuft über Google Play Billing beim Kauf.

Summary

No user accounts, no login, no registration, no email is collected in the app. No server connection for the core features (tasks, points, history, settings) – everything stays exclusively on the device. No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. The only outbound connection is Google Play Billing at the time of purchase.

Data processing in detail

Data categoryStorage locationNote
Tasks/notes (free text)Local only (app storage on the device)May contain any text; we do not read or transmit it
SettingsLocal onlyLanguage, reminder times, break schedule
History/statisticsLocal onlyAutomatically limited to the last 120 days
Frequency shortlist for suggestionsLocal onlyLimited to 400 entries
Purchase/unlock statusLocal onlyJust one boolean plus two timestamps, no payment data
Groups/friends featureLocal onlyCurrently a pure simulation with no server – displayed members are locally generated placeholders; no real third-party personal data is processed

Recipients

When purchasing full access, payment is processed entirely via Google Play Billing. We only receive information on whether a product was purchased – no credit card or account data.

Device permissions

INTERNET (exclusively for Google Play Billing), VIBRATE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK (for local reminders). No location, camera, microphone, contacts or file storage permission, no advertising ID.

Automatic device backup

If your device uses Android's automatic backup, the operating system may back up the locally stored app data, encrypted, to your Google account. This is a function of your device, not a transfer to us.

International data transfers

These legal pages are hosted via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (publicly viewable at dataprivacyframework.gov/participant/6174), recognised by the EU Commission as a safe legal framework via its adequacy decision of 10 July 2023.

Retention and deletion

History is automatically deleted after 120 days; the frequency shortlist is limited to 400 entries. Complete deletion of all data occurs by uninstalling the app or via "Clear storage/data" in the Android system settings.
